# HR AI Suite

A collection of smart tools designed to help individuals grow their careers while enabling companies and HR teams to attract talent and manage recruitment more effectively.

## Available Projects

- AI Interview Simulator
- PassATS AI
- PolyCV AI
- ProfileUp AI
- Smart Recruiters AI
- HR Brief AI
